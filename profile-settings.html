Help

Find answers, get in touch, or send us feedback.

How do I deactivate my account?
Go to Account Settings → Deactivate Account. Deactivation is temporary — your data is preserved and you can reactivate by logging back in at any time.
How do I edit a post?
Navigate to your Profile, find the post you want to edit, tap the three-dot menu (⋯) on the post card, then select "Edit Post".
How do I reset my password?
Head to Account Settings → Password & Security. Enter your current password and choose a new one. If you've forgotten it, use "Forgot Password" on the login screen.
How do I connect Google Health?
Go to Privacy Settings → Connected Apps and toggle Google Health API on. You'll be prompted to authorise the connection via your Google account.
Can I change my email address?
Email addresses are linked to your account identity and cannot be changed directly. Please contact Support if you need assistance.
